# Use the keyboard

This page helps you reach your panels and buttons without the mouse.

## Bring panels to front

1. Press **Ctrl+Shift+Alt+M**. Every panel comes above your other windows.
2. Pick a button with the arrow keys and press Enter to open it. The panels go back behind your windows.
3. To send them back without opening anything, press **Ctrl+Shift+Alt+M** again, or Esc.

You can also choose **Bring panels to front**, the first item in the menu of the MDSHO icon in the taskbar's
notification area.

## Change the shortcut

1. Open a panel's **Settings** and choose **Keyboard**.
2. Click the box under **Bring panels to front**, then press the new shortcut. Use Ctrl, Alt or Win with a key.
3. Click **Apply**.

Click **Default** for Ctrl+Shift+Alt+M, or **Off** for no shortcut. If another app already uses the shortcut, MDSHO
says so; choose another one.

## Keys in a panel

Select a button first, with a click or the arrow keys.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Enter | Opens the button, or folds or unfolds a group. Does nothing on a snippet; double-click it to paste. |
| F2 | Renames the button. |
| Delete | Deletes the button, after asking. |
| Ctrl+X | Cuts the button. |
| Ctrl+C | Copies the button. |
| Ctrl+V | Pastes into the selected group, or into the group the selected button is in. |

The right-click menu of a button shows these keys next to Rename, Cut, Copy, Paste and Delete.

## Rename

1. Select a button or group and press F2, or right-click it and choose **Rename**.
2. Type the new name.
3. Press Enter to keep it, or Esc to cancel.

With the mouse, click a button or group, wait a moment, and click it again, like in Explorer (not in a panel set to
**Single click to open**). A fast double click still opens it.

To rename a panel, right-click its title and choose **Rename window title**, or click the title text twice slowly.

## Prompts and dialogs

Prompts, such as **Delete window**, work like a Windows message box:

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Left, Right | Moves between the buttons. The focus starts on the default button, for example No when deleting. |
| Y, N, C, O | Answers Yes, No, Cancel or OK (the underlined letter). Alt with the letter works too. |
| Enter | Presses the button with the focus. |
| Esc | Answers Cancel, or No when there is no Cancel. |

In **Settings**, **Colours** and **About**:

- Tab and Shift+Tab move between the controls; the focused button shows a blue ring.
- In a group of options, such as **Window theme**, the arrow keys choose the option.
- Enter clicks **Apply** in Settings and Colours.
- Esc closes the window. In Settings and Colours, nothing is kept unless you click Apply.

## Notes and comments

- In a note, Esc closes the note. It is already saved.
- In a comment or the snippet editor, Esc or Ctrl+Enter closes the window. What you typed is already saved.
- Alt+F opens the **More options** menu (Text history, Wrap text, Font and more) of a note, comment or snippet.
  Shift+F10 or the Menu key opens the menu of the text (Cut, Copy, Paste and, in a note, Highlight).
