# Make your first panel

This page helps you fill a panel with buttons for the files, programs, folders and web sites you use most.

## Add files, programs and folders

1. Open File Explorer next to your panel.
2. Drag one or more files, programs or folders onto the panel.
3. Each one gets its own button. A folder button opens the folder in File Explorer.

To put a button inside a group, drop it on the group's name.

> Some items, such as Control Panel items, can't be dropped directly. Drag them to the desktop first to make a
> shortcut, then drag that shortcut onto the panel.

## Add a web link

1. In your browser, select the address in the address bar.
2. Drag it onto the panel.
3. The new button opens that address in your browser.

The button is named after the page or link title when the browser gives one, otherwise after the address.

## Open a button

1. Click or double-click the button. Which one depends on **Single click to open**, in the panel's **Settings** on the
   **General** page.
2. Hover over a button to see what it opens.

## Sort buttons into groups

1. Right-click an empty part of the panel and choose **New group**.
2. Right-click the new group, choose **Rename**, type a name and press Enter.
3. Drag buttons onto the group's name to move them into it.
4. Open a group the same way as a button to fold it or unfold it.

## Tidy up

- **Rename** a button: right-click it and choose **Rename**, select it and press F2, or click it, wait a moment, and
  click it again. The slow click doesn't rename in a panel set to **Single click to open**. **Set name the same as
  target** gives it the name of what it opens.
- **Move** a button: drag it, or use **Cut** and **Paste** from its right-click menu.
- **Comment** on a button: right-click it and choose **Comment...**. The comment shows in its tooltip. It saves itself
  while you type and when you close the window. The title bar has **Text size**, **Font** and **H** (Text history: the
  two earlier versions of the comment, to put one back), like a note.
- **Delete** a button: right-click it and choose **Delete**. The file it opens is not deleted.

## Add another panel

1. Right-click the panel's title and choose **New window**.
2. The new panel opens in the middle of the same screen. Drag it by its title to where you want it.
3. To name it, right-click its title and choose **Rename window title**, or click the title text, wait a moment, and
   click it again.

The first time you start MDSHO, its panel has a **Getting started** group with a sample of each kind of button. Delete
the group when you are done.
