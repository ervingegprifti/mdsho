# Change the look

This page helps you choose the colours of your panels, the style of the icons and the theme of MDSHO's own windows.

## Colour a panel

1. Click **Colours** in the panel's title bar.
2. Pick a part on the left: **Border**, **Title background**, **Title text**, **Background** or **Text**.
3. Choose a colour in the palette. The panel changes as you pick.
4. Click **Apply**. Closing the window without Apply puts the old colours back.

To try ready-made colours, click one of the **Presets** under the colours: three for each season, such as Winter: Frost
or Summer: Ocean. It sets all five colours at once; click **Apply** to keep them. New panels start with Autumn:
Halloween, a black title bar over a partly see-through black body.

A panel's clones share its colours.

## Save your own default colours

1. Colour a panel the way you like.
2. In its **Colours** window, click **Set as default**. New panels get these colours.
3. To give another panel these colours, open its **Colours** window, click **Default colours**, then **Apply**.

Notes have their own colours and defaults. See [Keep notes beside your work](notes.md).

## Mark a button with a colour

1. Right-click a button and point to **Mark**.
2. Choose a coloured mark. The empty first item, **No mark**, removes it.

## Choose solid or outlined icons

1. Open a panel's **Settings** and choose **Appearance**.
2. Under **Icons appearance**, choose **Solid icons** or **Outlined icons**.
3. Click **Apply**.

## Choose the window theme

MDSHO's own windows, comments and menus follow Windows' light or dark mode. To choose:

1. Open a panel's **Settings** and choose **Appearance**.
2. Under **Window theme**, choose **Same as Windows**, **Light** or **Dark**. You see it as you choose.
3. Click **Apply**.

The theme is for this PC. Panels and notes keep their own colours.

> In **Settings** on the **General** page, **Show version in title** adds the MDSHO version after the panel's title.
