# Keep notes beside your work

This page helps you keep short notes on a panel and open them in their own window while you work.

## Make a note

1. Right-click an empty part of the panel, or a group, and choose **New note**.
2. The note opens in its own window. Type your text.
3. The note saves itself as you type. Close it with its close button or Esc.

## Open and close a note

1. Open the note's button the same way as any other button. The note opens in its own window.
2. Open the button again to close the note.
3. You can also right-click the button and choose **Edit note...**.

Hover over a note's button to see its first lines.

## Name a note

1. Click the note's title in the note window, wait a moment, and click it again.
2. Type the new name and press Enter.

You can also select the button and press F2.

## Keep a note on top

1. Click **Keep on top** in the note's title bar. The note stays above your other windows.
2. Click it again (**Stop keeping on top**) to let other windows cover it.

Click **Wrap text** to fit long lines to the window's width.

## Change the text size

The **Text size** button left of **Font** shows the note's size in points. Click it and pick a size from 8 to 72 pt
(the same sizes as in Word), or **Default (12 pt)**; the current one has a tick. You can also hold Ctrl and turn the
mouse wheel to step through the same sizes, and the button follows. The note keeps its size. New notes start at 12 pt; other notes keep the size saved
with them.

## Go back to earlier text

A note keeps two earlier versions of its text. When you start changing it after opening the note, or when one change
removes a lot (half the text or more), the text you had becomes **Before**, and **Before** becomes
**Earlier**.

1. Click **H** (Text history) in the note's title bar.
2. Each version shows when it was kept and its first line. Pick **Before** or **Earlier**.

The note shows that text and saves it. The text you had becomes **Before**, so you can pick it back.

## Pick a font

1. Click **Font** in the note's title bar.
2. Pick a font. Each name shows in its own font, and the current one has a check mark.

The note changes at once and keeps its font. New notes start in Courier New. **From Windows** lists Courier New,
Consolas, Arial, Verdana and Calibri; **Comes with MDSHO** lists fonts that are part of MDSHO. Copying text from a note still copies
plain text, without the font.

## Colour a note

1. Click **Note colours** in the note's title bar.
2. Pick a part on the left: **Border**, **Title background**, **Title text**, **Background** or **Text**.
3. Choose a colour in the palette. The note changes as you pick.
4. Click **Apply**. Closing the window without Apply puts the old colours back.

The season **Presets** under the colours set all five at once; click **Apply** to keep them.

Click **Set as default** to use these colours for new notes, and **Default colours** to go back to them.
