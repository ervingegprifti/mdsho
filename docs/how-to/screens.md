# Use several screens

This page helps you show panels on more than one screen, dock them to a screen edge, and find a panel you can't see.

## Show a panel on every screen

1. Click **Clone this window to all screens** in the panel's title bar, or right-click the title and choose it there.
2. A clone of the panel opens in the middle of each screen that doesn't show it yet.
3. A change on one clone, such as a new button or a new colour, shows on all of them.

To remove a clone, click **Close this clone** in its title bar. The panel and its other clones stay.

## Dock a panel to a screen edge

1. Click **Dock window left** or **Dock window right** in the panel's title bar.
2. The panel moves to that edge of its screen and fills its height.
3. To undock it, drag it away by its title.

## Panels go back when screens change

MDSHO remembers where your panels and notes were for each set of connected screens. Undock your laptop and dock it
again, and they go back where they were with those screens.

1. Right-click the MDSHO icon in the taskbar's notification area and point to **Screen setups**.
2. The list shows the setups MDSHO knows; the one in use has a tick.
3. Choose **Put panels back for this setup** to move them back now.
4. Choose **Forget the other setups** to keep only the one in use.

To turn this off, open a panel's **Settings**, choose **Screens** and clear **Remember places for each screen setup**,
then click **Apply**.

## Find a lost panel

If a panel is off screen or hidden:

1. Right-click the MDSHO icon in the taskbar's notification area.
2. Choose **Find MDSHO lost windows**. Every panel and clone moves to the middle of the main screen.

Double-click a panel's title to move it to the middle of its own screen.

> **Find other lost windows** in the same menu helps with other apps: it moves their windows whose title bar is off
> screen to the screen with the mouse pointer.
